Quantum Advantage

Solving portfolio optimization on a quantum computer

Two quantum algorithms attack this problem. HHL solves the Markowitz linear system A·w = b for the optimal asset weights in time scaling with log N — exponentially faster than any classical solver's N. The Quantum Stochastic Walk explores the asset-correlation graph as an open quantum system, spreading ballistically where a classical random walk only diffuses. HHL — Quantum Linear Solver

The Harrow–Hassidim–Lloyd algorithm prepares the solution of A·w = b as a quantum state in O(log N · κ²/ε) time. The best classical solvers need O(N · κ · log 1/ε) — linear in the number of assets. Slide the problem size to watch the operation counts diverge.

Quantum Stochastic Walk — Quantum vs. Classical Exploration

A walker starts on the highest-Sharpe asset and explores the correlation graph under a GKLS (Lindblad) master equation. The mixing knob ω interpolates between a coherent quantum walk (ω→0) and a classical random walk (ω→1). Quantum coherence lets it spread faster and build structure that decoherence washes out.
